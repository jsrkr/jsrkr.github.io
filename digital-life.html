05 About

About the Digital Life Atlas

The Atlas brings together three measured dimensions of digital life and summarizes them with the Digital Life Index. Figure 3 compares one state's normalized profile; Figure 4 shows selected underlying statistics for all states and DC in their original units.

Digital Work
The share of workers usually working from home, shown as a standardized input in the index and as a percentage in Figure 4.
Digital Leisure
The updated source reports average digital leisure minutes by state and year. Its data file does not provide a more specific activity or sample definition.
Digital Accessibility
An equal-weight average of standardized household internet, high-speed internet, and cellular data-plan shares.

06 Method

Method

The updated source covers 2016-2019 and 2021-2023 for all 50 states and DC. Digital Work uses z_remote, standardized from the ACS remote_share; the inspected ACS code defines this as the survey-weighted share of workers age 16 or older who usually work from home. Digital Leisure uses z_leisure, standardized from the supplied digital_leisure_minutes_mean. That raw field has no Stata variable label, and the inspected construction code does not document a more specific activity or sample definition. Digital Accessibility is digital_access, the arithmetic mean of z_internet, z_highspeed, and z_cellular, standardized from household shares. The cellular share is named share_cellular_data_plan in the updated source.

The Digital Life Index and its component scores are relative measures normalized between 0 and 1 over the pooled state-year sample. These scores are not percentages. Figure 1-3 use the stored index and three component display fields. The component scores are independently min-max normalized; they are not direct physical quantities. Figure 4 presents selected underlying measures in their original units, including percentages of households or workers and average daily minutes of digital leisure. Its cellular-data measure is stored in the updated source as share_cellular_data_plan.

The CSV adds digital_work_score_01, digital_leisure_score_01, and digital_accessibility_score_01. Each is calculated from the updated pooled sample as (source measure - pooled minimum) / (pooled maximum - pooled minimum). The finalized index is not reconstructed for display. See the methods README for source fields, exact pooled extrema, and variable definitions.

ATUS did not produce a comparable full-year estimate for 2020, so it is omitted rather than interpolated.
